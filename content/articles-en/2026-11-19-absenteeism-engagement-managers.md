Today, November 19, 2026, the release of Grand Theft Auto VI will likely empty a few offices in Quebec. Some employees requested time off. Others simply called in sick.

The story makes people smile. It also illustrates something every manager deals with: a wave of short absences that are hard to verify. What the law allows you to do is limited. What these absences reveal is often more useful.

### Medical notes: what the Act respecting labour standards says

Since January 1, 2025, Bill 68 has amended the Act respecting labour standards. Employers can no longer require supporting documents, including a medical note, for the first three absences of three consecutive days or less within a 12-month period.

Employers may once again request supporting documents in two cases:

•       from the fourth short absence within the same 12-month period;

•       as soon as an absence exceeds three consecutive days.

The Act also provides that the first two days of sick leave each year are paid, for employees with at least three months of continuous service.

These rules are a minimum. A collective agreement or an internal policy can offer employees more, never less.

### Mistakes to avoid

**Hunting for evidence on social media.** Viewing an employee's public posts is not prohibited. The line lies elsewhere: accessing a private profile through a fake account or through a colleague raises privacy concerns. And a post does not always prove what you think it does. Checking systematically after every absence mainly damages trust.

**Applying the rules unevenly.** An absence policy must apply to everyone in the same way. Tolerance for some and strictness for others opens the door to complaints.

**Disciplining without evidence.** A false claim of illness can be misconduct. But it has to be proven. Without evidence, a disciplinary measure is likely to be overturned.

**Responding collectively to individual behaviour.** Tightening the rules for the whole team because of a few absences penalizes those who play fair, and has little effect on the people the rules are meant to target.

### Absenteeism as an indicator of engagement

The same event produces different results from one team to the next. Some stay at full strength. Others empty out.

The difference rarely lies in the event. It lies in the climate.

An engaged employee plans their absence. They book time off and make arrangements with their colleagues. A disengaged employee is absent without notice. Often, this is not bad faith. They simply do not feel comfortable asking.

Occasional absenteeism acts like a thermometer. It does not create the problem. It makes it visible.

### Three questions for managers

1.      Do my employees feel comfortable asking me for time off?

2.      Do I approve enough requests for them to dare to ask?

3.      Does my team have enough slack to absorb an absence?

A no to any of these questions deserves more attention than the absence itself.

### Frequently asked questions

**Can I require a medical note for a one-day absence?** Not for the first three absences of three days or less within 12 months. From the fourth one on, yes.

**Can I refuse a leave request submitted in advance?** Yes, depending on the organization's needs and the rules in your policy or collective agreement. The criteria for granting leave must be clear and applied consistently to everyone.

**Do these rules apply to all businesses?** They cover employees governed by Quebec's Act respecting labour standards. Federally regulated businesses, such as banks, telecommunications and interprovincial transportation, fall under the Canada Labour Code.
